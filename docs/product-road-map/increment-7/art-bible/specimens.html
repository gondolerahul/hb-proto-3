§2.1

The gold budget

Gold means one of exactly two things: this needs you, or this is certified. On a still estate with nothing to do, there is almost no gold on screen — which is what makes the first beacon unmissable.

What the budget forbids. Active states, healthy KPIs, ordinary headings, links, chart series and colleague portraits at rest are all built from the warm-white ramp (--fg → --fg-muted → --fg-subtle → --fg-faint), never gold. Five ambient steps, no meaning of their own — which is exactly what a world needs for its mass.

§4 · charter decision 3

Day–night, as luminance

The territory follows the tenant's local time. Under the brand palette that is a change of light, not of colour — the frame is near-black at every hour, and the beacons read identically in both, as spec §4 requires.

Night is the brand's default look, so night is also the cheaper render — the low-power device tier and the on-brand tier are the same tier.

§5 · law L6

The Glasshouse: silvered without a second hue

The twin is the real, desaturated — not recoloured. It introduces no hue, it is the correct metaphor (a simulation is the world with the life taken out), and because the renderer applies it at the plane boundary, a twin-derived component cannot be styled to look real. Gold survives inside the Glasshouse for exactly two things: the divergence ribbon, and certified seals on the promotion pipeline.

Honesty grade · forecast

Raising the follow-up cadence to every 4 days is projected to recover ₹1.4L a month.

Forecast — seasonal-naive with drift over 11 weeks of history. Not a replay; the projection has never happened.

Every Glasshouse-derived component carries its grade, and the schema makes honesty_grade mandatory — the manifest layer refuses one without it (L6, D4 §Certified rules). Planning adds a fourth value TWIN does not have: untested — never tried — which must never render like unknown — tried, ungradable.

§7 · law L7 · spec §14.5

Portraits — four directions

The brand's one ornament is a halftone of gold dots. Making that the portrait medium means the artwork itself carries L7's disclosure: a dot-screen bust is unmistakably synthetic at a glance, in every context, with no badge that a card layout could crop away.

Recommendation: A as the house style, C as the automatic fallback — a colleague with a defined persona gets a bust; a gateway, a Meta-Agent role, a newly seeded agent or a colleague in the Gallery gets a seal generated from its id. Nothing is ever portrait-less and nothing waits on an art pipeline.

VP-02, for R1: direction C has no production cost at all — no ADC, no illustrator, no raster assets, and it cannot drift from a colleague's versions. That may make it the floor rather than the fallback. The honest counter is that a room of seals is a filing cabinet, and the dossier surfaces are built on the premise that you meet someone.

§8 · WCAG 2.2 AA

Weather — texture, motion, icon, sentence

Weather carries health information and under a one-hue palette it cannot lean on colour at all. Each state is texture + motion + an icon + a sentence, and the last two are what the sheet equivalent (L9) and the screen reader receive.

In a storm the real signal is that the traffic stops, not that there is a cloud. Which is exactly why every row also has an icon and a sentence: the reduced-motion path removes the channel doing most of the work, and it must lose atmosphere without losing information.

§6

Type — and the missing serif

Spec §11 asked for "one serif of real character" for narrative surfaces. It is retired with the rest of §11. The narrative register is Hanken Grotesk at 1.7 line-height on a 60–72ch measure — the brand achieves "calm, confident, quietly profound" with a neutral humanist sans plus air, and a serif would have been a fifth face and a second personality.
