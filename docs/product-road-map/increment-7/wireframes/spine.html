Depth 0 has no chrome because it is the chrome. At zero hands raised there is no gold anywhere on the screen — that is the whole design (art bible §2.1).
The shell is app-owned, not manifest-composed. A malformed or hostile manifest cannot remove the still line, the depth dial or the way out of a surface.
The countdown never alarms. It ticks, and that is all it does. An SLA that shouts turns a considered decision into a rushed one.
The certified block is byte-identical in the tray, on the Line and in a sheet (L5). Everything above the gold rule may differ by density; nothing below it may.
